About Liblu Blaan

A small step toward language preservation.

Liblu Blaan ay isang digital na proyektong naglalayong magtipon at magbahagi ng mga salita, tunog, kuwento, folklore, at kaalaman tungkol sa pamana ng Blaan. Nilikha ito bilang lugar na maaaring balikan, matutuhan, at patuloy na pagyamanin.

“Blaan ang tribo, Blaan din ang lengwahe ko.”

Created by: Floramae Malayon

Language and cultural entries should be shared respectfully, with clear source attribution and consultation with knowledgeable community members.

Site policies

Terms, Privacy & Content Policy

Guidelines for using Liblu Blaan. The site owner should ensure that the privacy notice matches actual data practices before treating it as a final legal notice.

Terms of Use

Use this website for learning, sharing knowledge, and respecting Blaan language and culture. Do not post illegal, abusive, misleading, spam, private information about others, or material you do not have permission to share. The owner may remove content that violates these rules. Language and cultural entries should include source attribution and verification whenever possible.

Privacy Policy

The website may process owner email/session data for authentication, along with display names, comments, reactions, and submitted posts. This data supports community features, site security, and content moderation. Do not post passwords, government IDs, financial details, or sensitive personal information in comments. Supabase and GitHub Pages are third-party providers. Contact the site owner to request access, correction, or removal. Do not promise that tracking is absent without checking provider settings. The owner should ensure this policy matches actual data practices and applicable law before publishing it as a final legal notice.

Copyright & Cultural Respect

Credit authors, storytellers, photographers, and sources. Do not copy or republish protected material without permission. Respect knowledge that is restricted from public sharing. Posting material does not transfer its copyright to the website.
